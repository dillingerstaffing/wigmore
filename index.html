The schema.
Wigmore's chart method has a fixed vocabulary: four shapes, four lines, a handful of marks. This page is the whole vocabulary. The builder enforces it; nothing else can be drawn.

After J.H. Wigmore, The Principles of Judicial Proof (1913) and The Science of Judicial Proof (1937), via the key recovered by Jean Goodwin and the simplified conventions of Anderson (1999).
